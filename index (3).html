One artist. Start to finish.

The same hands that did your last set do your next one. Ask a big salon to promise that.

Sani Touch is Sanaz Eghbali's one-artist studio inside A&S Beauty Bar. Certified in lash and permanent-makeup work, licensed in California, and at the chair for every minute of every appointment. Every tool comes out of a sealed pouch in front of you, and nothing is rushed.

Questions

Asked honestly, answered plainly.

Will she actually match the design I bring?

Bring your inspo photo; we look at it together, and she tells you what your nails can carry before a single tool touches you, not after. The matched pairs above are that promise, kept.

Is it actually clean?

Every tool is opened from a sealed sterilized pouch in front of you, every appointment. Cleanliness here is something you watch happen, not a sentence you read.

Is she really trained for Russian manicure?

The work answers that: color seated at the very base, edges finished under magnification, process on camera on the grid. Certificates hang at the station, and the technique is dry, precise, and never rushed.

How long does an appointment take?

From about an hour to two and a half hours depending on the service. The time is the point; rushed is what you are leaving behind, and exact times show at booking.

Where is it, and what about parking?

Inside A&S Beauty Bar at 19944 1/2 Ventura Blvd, Woodland Hills, CA 91364, with plenty of free parking in the shopping center lot. Look for the beauty bar; her chair is inside.

Will we be able to communicate?

Bring the photo; hands speak fluent design. English works at the chair, and Farsi is warmly welcome.
